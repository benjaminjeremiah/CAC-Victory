About CAC Victory District

Our History

Christ Apostolic Church Victory District has been serving the community for many years, providing spiritual guidance, fellowship, and support to believers of all walks of life.

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, make disciples, and serve our community with love, compassion, and integrity. We believe in the power of faith, prayer, and fellowship to transform lives.

Our Vision

To establish a thriving community of believers who are committed to spiritual growth, evangelism, and community service. We envision a church that reflects the love and grace of Jesus Christ in all we do.

Our Core Values

  • Faith: We believe in the power of God and His Word
  • Love: We practice unconditional love toward all people
  • Service: We serve our community with humility and dedication
  • Integrity: We conduct ourselves with honesty and transparency
  • Community: We foster strong bonds among believers

Leadership

Our church is led by dedicated pastors and leaders who are committed to the spiritual growth and welfare of our congregation. We welcome you to meet our leadership team.
